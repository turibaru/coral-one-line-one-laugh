## The line

> The recipe said 'serves four,' so I ate it alone in the dark and now I understand why it needed witnesses.

The joke opens as ordinary domestic comedy — a recipe's serving size, nothing unusual — then "ate it alone in the dark" quietly tips it over, and "needed witnesses" detonates it: the portion wasn't a suggestion, it was a safety measure. Three beats, no filler, 106 characters, and a turn the reader only sees coming once it has already landed.

## How it runs

`main.ts` reads `entry.txt` at runtime and copies it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. It exits 1 when the entry is empty, whitespace-only, or longer than 200 characters, and 0 when the entry is valid.
